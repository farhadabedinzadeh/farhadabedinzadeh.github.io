Farhad Abedinzadeh is a PhD researcher in Bioengineering at Durham University, working at the intersection of artificial intelligence (AI), medical imaging, and ophthalmology. His current research focuses on developing interpretable AI methods to analyse retinal imaging and ocular biometric data, aiming to improve early prediction of childhood myopia.

Understanding change.
Predicting earlier.

PhD in Bioengineering · Durham University

Interpretable AI for early prediction of childhood myopia

My PhD investigates longitudinal, multimodal retinal imaging and biometric data, with the aim of identifying changes that may precede the onset of myopia in children.

I am interested in models that capture how an eye changes over time, combine complementary measurements and offer explanations that can be assessed against clinical knowledge.

Research foundations

My previous research focused on neurodevelopmental and neurodegenerative disorders, using AI to develop prototype computer-aided diagnosis systems. My research interests centre on clinically meaningful AI methods that improve our understanding, prediction and assessment of human health.
